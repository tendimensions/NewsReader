# Suggested Technology RSS Feeds

Ten popular technology RSS feeds to consider adding to the reader.
None of these overlap with the current built-in feeds (Ars Technica, The Verge, TechCrunch, Hacker News, Wired).

| # | Name | RSS URL | Notes |
|---|------|---------|-------|
| 1 | **MIT Technology Review** | `https://www.technologyreview.com/feed/` | In-depth tech & science journalism from MIT |
| 2 | **IEEE Spectrum** | `https://spectrum.ieee.org/feeds/feed.rss` | Engineering and applied science from the IEEE |
| 3 | **Engadget** | `https://www.engadget.com/rss.xml` | Consumer tech news and reviews |
| 4 | **9to5Mac** | `https://9to5mac.com/feed/` | Apple-focused news, leaks, and how-tos |
| 5 | **Android Authority** | `https://www.androidauthority.com/feed/` | Android devices, apps, and Google ecosystem |
| 6 | **Tom's Hardware** | `https://www.tomshardware.com/feeds/all` | Hardware reviews, benchmarks, and PC building |
| 7 | **Slashdot** | `https://rss.slashdot.org/Slashdot/slashdotMain` | Long-running community tech news aggregator |
| 8 | **The Register** | `https://www.theregister.com/headlines.atom` | British IT news with a sharp editorial voice |
| 9 | **Lobsters** | `https://lobste.rs/rss` | Invite-only link aggregator — high signal-to-noise tech discussions |
| 10 | **CNET News** | `https://www.cnet.com/rss/news/` | Broad consumer tech and gadget coverage |

## How to add a feed

1. Open the app and tap the **Settings** gear icon (top-right of the feed)
2. Scroll to **Feed Sources** and tap **Add Feed**
3. Paste the RSS URL from the table above and give it a name
4. Tap **Save** — the feed will appear in your list immediately
